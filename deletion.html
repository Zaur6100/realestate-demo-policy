How to make a request

  1. Email the privacy contact above and state that you want your demo data deleted.
  2. Include the WhatsApp phone number you used, in international format, and approximate dates of your test conversation so the operator can locate the relevant records.
  3. Specify whether your request concerns all demo records or a particular conversation. The operator may ask for limited confirmation that the records concern you.

Passwords, API access tokens, and Meta app secrets are not needed for a deletion request.

What happens next

The operator will locate relevant records in the local n8n execution history and any operator-held debugging copies, remove identifiable data covered by your request unless an applicable legal exception requires limited retention, and confirm the outcome by email.

You will receive a response within one month. If an extension is permitted and needed, the operator will explain it within the first month.

Scope of deletion

This process concerns records held by the demo's operator. Copies in your own WhatsApp chat and records independently held by WhatsApp/Meta, Cloudflare, or other providers are managed through those providers' own privacy tools and policies.

See the privacy policy for the data handled by the current demo and the services involved.
